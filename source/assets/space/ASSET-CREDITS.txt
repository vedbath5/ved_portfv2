Planet surface maps: NASA/JPL-Caltech Solar System Simulator texture maps.
JPL texture map catalog: https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/
Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Venus, Neptune and Pluto map images are used as round planet surfaces; textures include fictional/processed maps as documented by JPL.
